A decentralized network owned by its operators scales exponentially faster. By allowing venues and players to invest via the Community Syndicate, we solve the cold-start problem of two-sided marketplaces.

A bar owner who buys into the network for $2,500 is infinitely more likely to keep the TV Node powered on and push the $5.00 CPA QR codes to patrons. They are an owner, not just a host.
